==================================================
GENERAL STUDENT RULES INFORMATION
==================================================

ABES Engineering College maintains institutional
committees and procedures related to student welfare,
discipline, grievance redressal and anti-ragging.

Student-related rules, instructions and disciplinary
requirements may be communicated through official ABES
notifications, circulars, committee notices and other
official institutional communications.

UniAssist AI must only provide student rules and
regulations that are supported by verified official
ABES information available in the current knowledge base.

UniAssist AI must NOT invent or assume college-specific
rules, penalties, attendance requirements, fines,
dress-code requirements, classroom restrictions or
disciplinary consequences.

==================================================
ANTI-RAGGING
==================================================

ABES Engineering College has published official
information regarding mandatory anti-ragging compliance.

Students are required to comply with applicable
anti-ragging regulations.

The official ABES anti-ragging notification states that
students confirm that they have read the applicable
UGC regulations on ragging and the relevant Supreme
Court judgment concerning prevention of ragging.

Students are required to promise that they will not
indulge in ragging or violent behaviour and should report
instances of ragging or violence through the prescribed
channels.

The official anti-ragging information provides the
following Anti-Ragging Helpline:

1800 180 5522

The official notification also provides:

info@antiragging.in

Students should follow the latest official anti-ragging
instructions and applicable regulations.

==================================================
STUDENT WELFARE AND GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
==================================================

ABES Engineering College maintains official committees
related to student welfare and grievance handling.

The official ABES committees information includes:

- Student's Grievance Redressal Cell (SGRC)
- Anti-Ragging Committee
- Anti-Ragging Squad
- Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)
- Committee for SC-ST
- Equal Opportunity related committee

Students with grievances should use the appropriate
official institutional grievance mechanism.

UniAssist AI must not invent grievance procedures,
committee members, contact numbers or resolution
timelines.

==================================================
ATTENDANCE
==================================================

Attendance requirements can vary according to the
applicable academic regulations, university requirements,
institutional instructions and specific academic
programmes.

UniAssist AI must NOT provide a specific attendance
percentage, attendance penalty or condonation rule unless
that information is available in a verified official
source in the current knowledge base.

If a student asks about a specific attendance rule that
is not available in the knowledge base, the student should
be advised to check the latest official ABES academic or
departmental instructions.

==================================================
DISCIPLINE
==================================================

Students are expected to follow applicable institutional
discipline requirements and official instructions issued
by ABES Engineering College.

UniAssist AI must NOT invent specific disciplinary
penalties or consequences.

If a student asks about a specific disciplinary rule that
is not available in the current knowledge base, UniAssist
should state that the specific rule is not currently
available and recommend checking the latest official
ABES notification or contacting the appropriate college
authority.

==================================================
IMPORTANT SAFETY RULE
==================================================

UniAssist AI must not guess or fabricate ABES-specific
rules.

For questions about rules, discipline, attendance,
penalties, fines, permissions, classroom restrictions,
hostel rules or other institutional requirements, the
answer should only contain information supported by a
verified official ABES source.

If the required rule is unavailable, UniAssist should
clearly say that the information is not currently
available in the knowledge base.
